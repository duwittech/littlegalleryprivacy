Keep every drawing they bring home.

Photograph the artwork, and littleGallery files it by date and remembers how old they were when they made it. Months later the timeline reads like a story.

Get it on Google Play Coming to iPhone
Android 8 and up, free to start. The iPhone version is on its way — tell us if you would like a note when it lands.

What it does

  • Capture in seconds Snap the drawing or import a photo you already took, then crop it in a step built for flat paper.
  • Filed by age, automatically Every piece carries the date and how old the artist was that day. The timeline groups itself by month.
  • Their voice, kept Record them telling you about the drawing, and the memo stays attached to the picture.
  • A gallery each Every child gets their own wall, their own banner, their own story so far.

It stays on your phone

littleGallery has no account to create and no cloud to sync with. The photos, the recordings and your child's name live in the app's own storage on your device, and nothing is uploaded to us — we have no way to see any of it.

No ads. No tracking. No AI looking at your child's drawings. The whole app works offline.

Support

Something broken, or an idea for the next version? Write to duwittechnologies@gmail.com and a person will read it.

If you are reporting a problem, it helps to include your phone model, its Android or iOS version, and what you were doing when it happened.

Managing a subscription

littleGallery Plus is billed by the store you bought it from, and only the store can change or cancel it:

  • Android — Play Store app → your profile picture → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions.
  • iPhone — Settings → tap your name → Subscriptions.

Cancelling stops the renewal; Plus stays on until the period you have paid for ends, and everything you have already saved is yours to keep either way.
